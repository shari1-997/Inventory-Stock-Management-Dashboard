# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and professional.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and courteous.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Focus discussions on the project and its technical content.
- Respect different approaches and viewpoints.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful comments.
- Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Sharing private or sensitive information.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when conduct does not meet these standards.
